# GTO 0.6.0 Quests

0.6's quest chapters are written in code, in GTOCore's
`data/quest/chapter/New*Chapter.java`, and cover Steam through MAX (checked 2026-11-07).
The world keeps quest progress in `data/gtocore_quests.dat`, beside FTB Quests' own data.

For comparison, 0.5.6's book was complete through UHV, had 49 quests in UEV, and left UIV,
UXV and OpV as empty placeholders.

Quest text in the dev builds can be machine-translated from Chinese.
